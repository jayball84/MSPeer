A peer group for MSP leaders

Twelve operators.
One table.
No noise.

MSPeer brings founders and C-suite from serious managed service providers into one room — to share what's working, pressure-test the hard calls, and buy better together. Relaxed, honest, and capped at twelve.

Hosted by Jay Ball, CEO of Flotek Group, and Danny Mills, CRO of Everything Tech.

The point of it

Bring together MSPs who can genuinely share, support and benefit from one another. A peer group, not a sales pitch.

Who's in the room

The bar is high — and that's the whole point.

A peer group lives or dies on who's around the table. We keep the criteria tight so every conversation is worth your Thursday, and nobody's translating their world for outsiders.

M

Shared context means shared shorthand. Same fights, same vendors, same margins — no need to explain the basics.

✓

Where & how we meet

Around each other's boardroom tables.

We rotate the venue between partners. Whoever hosts opens the day with a walkthrough of their own business — the real version, not the website version.

The host

Rotating boardrooms

Each meeting lands in a different partner's boardroom. The host runs an intro and walkthrough of how they actually operate — the wins, the scars, the open questions.

The guest

One vendor, two hours

A vendor joins each meeting to introduce their company and products — and how they help MSPs — with up to a two-hour spot and special group pricing for the room.

The brief

Business insights

A short, timely session on something the room needs to know — Cyber Essentials changes, team benefits, regulation, whatever's moving.
